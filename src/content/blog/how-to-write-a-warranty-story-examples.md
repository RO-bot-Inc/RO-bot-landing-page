A warranty story gets paid the first time when it proves three things: the concern was real, the diagnosis was sound, and the repair followed the manufacturer's procedure. Everything else is detail. This post gives you a simple structure for the cause section, then 20 example stories across common repair categories that you can use to train technicians and advisors.

If you need a refresher on the basics, start with [What Are the 3Cs on a Repair Order?](/blog/what-are-the-3cs-on-a-repair-order/)

## The four-part cause that reviewers look for

Warranty reviewers and field auditors read thousands of stories. The ones that pass quickly follow the same pattern in the cause section:

1. **Verify.** Confirm the customer's concern and how you reproduced it.
2. **Test.** Name the tests and tools: scan data, pinpoint tests, pressure or voltage readings, visual inspection.
3. **Result.** Give the numbers, codes, and specifications.
4. **Root cause.** Name the failed part and how you know it caused the concern.

Then the correction states the repair, references the procedure or TSB, and confirms the fix.

## Engine and drivability

**1. Misfire.** Verified rough idle and flashing MIL. P0304 stored. Misfire counter isolated cylinder 4. Swapped coil 4 with coil 1; misfire stayed on cylinder 4. Swapped plug; misfire stayed. Injector balance test showed cylinder 4 injector outside specification. Root cause: failed fuel injector, cylinder 4. Replaced injector per procedure, cleared codes, road tested 10 miles, no misfires.

**2. Oil leak.** Verified oil residue at rear of engine. Cleaned area, added UV dye, ran engine 20 minutes. Dye traced to rear main seal area, no leak from oil pan or valve cover. Root cause: rear main seal leaking. Replaced seal per procedure, rechecked with UV light after test drive, no leak.

**3. Coolant loss.** Verified low coolant. Pressure tested system to specification; pressure dropped within five minutes. External leak found at water pump weep hole. Root cause: water pump seal failure. Replaced pump and gasket, filled and bled system, pressure test held.

**4. Hesitation on acceleration.** Verified hesitation from stop. No codes. Live data showed throttle position sensor signal dropout during tip-in. Wiggle test at connector reproduced dropout. Terminal tension test failed at TPS connector. Root cause: loose terminal in TPS connector. Repaired terminal per wiring repair procedure, road tested, no hesitation.

**5. Turbo noise.** Verified whistle under boost. Smoke tested intake tract; smoke escaping at charge pipe coupler. Coupler split. Root cause: cracked charge air coupler. Replaced coupler and clamp, smoke test passed, noise eliminated on road test.

## Transmission and driveline

**6. Harsh shift.** Verified harsh 2-3 upshift when warm. Checked fluid level and condition per procedure: correct level, no burnt odor. Found applicable TSB for software update. Root cause: shift calibration addressed by TSB. Performed software update per TSB, completed adaptive relearn, road tested, shifts normal.

**7. Clunk on takeoff.** Verified clunk from rear when accelerating from stop. Inspected on lift: excessive play in rear driveshaft U-joint. Root cause: worn U-joint. U-joint is not serviced separately. Replaced driveshaft assembly per procedure, road tested, noise gone.

**8. CV boot.** Verified grease on inner wheel well. Inspected front axle; outer CV boot torn, joint still quiet on turn test. Root cause: split outer CV boot. Replaced boot and clamps, repacked joint per procedure, verified no noise on full-lock turns.

## Electrical

**9. Dead battery.** Verified no-start, battery at 9.8 V. Charged and tested battery: passed. Parasitic draw test showed draw above specification after sleep. Pulled fuses to isolate: draw dropped with amplifier fuse removed. Root cause: amplifier not entering sleep mode. Replaced amplifier, retested draw within specification.

**10. Inoperative backup camera.** Verified black screen in reverse. Checked for codes: camera circuit open. Voltage at camera connector correct; camera not responding. Root cause: failed rearview camera. Replaced camera, calibrated per procedure, image displays correctly.

**11. Intermittent warning lights.** Verified multiple lights after key-on. Communication codes in several modules. Inspected network wiring; found chafed harness under seat track, shorted to ground. Root cause: harness damage from seat track contact. Repaired wiring per procedure, rerouted and secured harness, cleared codes, verified no returns over multiple key cycles.

**12. Key fob not recognized.** Verified "key not detected" message. Fob battery tested good. Second fob also not detected. Antenna resistance out of specification. Root cause: failed interior antenna. Replaced antenna, both fobs recognized.

## HVAC

**13. No cold air.** Verified vent temperature high. Gauges showed low side pressure low. Recovered refrigerant: below specification. Leak test with electronic detector found leak at condenser. Root cause: condenser leak. Replaced condenser, evacuated and recharged to specification, vent temperature within spec.

**14. Blower works on high only.** Verified low speeds inoperative. Tested blower resistor: open on lower speed circuits. Root cause: failed blower motor resistor. Replaced resistor, all speeds operate.

## Body, steering, and suspension

**15. Water leak.** Verified wet front carpet. Water test identified leak at windshield upper corner. Root cause: urethane seal gap. Reinstalled windshield per procedure, water tested, no leak.

**16. Steering pull.** Verified pull to the right. Tire pressures set to specification. Swapped front tires side to side; pull switched to left. Root cause: tire conicity. Replaced tire under tire warranty process, road tested, vehicle tracks straight.

**17. Front clunk over bumps.** Verified clunk on bump test. Inspected front suspension: excessive play in left lower control arm bushing. Root cause: worn bushing. Replaced lower control arm, performed alignment, road tested, noise gone.

**18. Door rattle.** Verified rattle in right rear door over rough pavement. Removed door trim; window regulator bolt loose. Root cause: regulator mounting bolt not torqued. Torqued to specification, applied anti-rattle tape, road tested, rattle eliminated.

## Could not duplicate and comebacks

**19. Could not duplicate.** Attempted to duplicate intermittent stall over two cold starts and a 25-mile road test. No codes. Freeze frame data not available. Checked for TSBs: none applicable. Could not duplicate concern. Advised customer to note conditions if it recurs. This story still documents diagnostic time and protects the store on a return visit.

**20. Repeat repair.** Vehicle returned with same misfire on cylinder 4 repaired on prior RO. Verified concern. Misfire followed the replacement injector when swapped. Root cause: replacement injector defective. Replaced injector, road tested, no misfires. Referenced prior RO number for warranty history.
