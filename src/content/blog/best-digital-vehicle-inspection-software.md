The best digital vehicle inspection (DVI) software for a dealership is the one your technicians will use on every repair order. Feature lists matter less than completion rate: a tool with every option that techs skip on busy days produces less revenue than a simple one they use on every car. This guide covers what DVI software does, the main tools franchise dealers compare, and the criteria that separate a good fit from software nobody uses.

## What digital vehicle inspection software does

DVI software replaces the paper multi-point inspection (MPI) sheet. At minimum it lets a technician:

- Record the condition of each inspection item, usually as green, yellow, or red.
- Attach photos, video, and measurements.
- Send the results to a service advisor, who builds a quote.
- Share the inspection with the customer by text or email for approval.

Better tools also store inspection history, report on completion and approval rates, and, where the DMS allows it, write findings back to the repair order.

## The DVI tools dealerships compare most

Vendor details are from each company's public materials as of November 2026.

### Xtime Inspect (Cox Automotive)

A digital MPI tool built into the Xtime service scheduling and service lane suite. A good fit for stores already running Xtime for scheduling, since inspection is one module of a larger workflow.

### CDK Inspect and the CDK Fixed Operations Suite

CDK's multipoint inspection tool supports technician photos and video, eSignature, and utilization reporting, and is part of CDK's Fixed Operations Suite. Since 2025, CDK has [offered the suite to dealers who don't run a CDK DMS](https://www.cdkglobal.com/media-center/cdk-expands-fixed-operations-suite-non-cdk-dms-customers-boosting-customer-service-and) as well.

### myKaarma ServiceCart Premium Video MPI

Video MPI inside myKaarma's broader communication and payments platform. It supports custom inspection workflows (express, recon, diesel, and others) and can auto-fill op codes and labor and parts pricing on recommendations. myKaarma has been [named a Ford Dealer Technology Preferred Partner for video](https://mykaarma.com/news/mykaarma-selected-as-ford-dealer-technology-preferred-partner-for-video-capability/), and its Tech Video Grader uses AI to check inspection video quality.

### TruVideo

A video-first texting and engagement platform for sales and service. Its inspection tool, TruInspect, lets technicians record findings in a color-coded template that goes straight to the advisor. In January 2026 TruVideo introduced its [Video Quality Score (VQS)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/truvideo-introduces-video-quality-score-vqs-302664894.html), which rates inspection video quality.

### Dealer-FX (Snap-on)

An OEM-endorsed service workflow platform, [owned by Snap-on](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210308005223/en/Snap-on-Acquires-Dealer-FX-Group-Inc.), with inspection as one module. Common at brands that have adopted it as an OEM program.

### Affinitiv MPI (formerly AutoLoop)

An MPI tool with OEM-tailored inspection forms, multiple inspection templates, and photo and video capture. Built for dealers using Affinitiv's scheduling and service lane products.

### AutoVitals

Inspection-to-approval is AutoVitals' core product. It is used mostly by independent and franchise repair shops, and less often by OEM-franchised new-car dealers.

### TenthGear

A voice-first platform built around the technician. Instead of tapping through screens with gloves on, techs speak their findings and [TenthGear](/product/) produces the [multi-point inspection](/product/#mpi), an [AI-guided inspection video](/product/#video) with an on-screen teleprompter, and the [3C warranty story](/product/#3cs), graded on warranty criteria. It integrates with ten DMS platforms, including CDK, Reynolds, Tekion, Dealertrack, and PBS.

## How the tools differ

- **Built into a suite vs. standalone.** Xtime, CDK, Affinitiv, and Dealer-FX treat inspection as one module of a scheduling or service lane suite. TruVideo and AutoVitals make inspection or video their main product.
- **Advisor-first vs. technician-first.** Most tools are built around the advisor's quote and the customer's approval. TenthGear starts in the bay, optimizing technician speed and accuracy.
- **Inspection only vs. inspection plus warranty.** Only a few tools also help with 3C stories and warranty documentation, which matters for franchise dealers with a large warranty mix.

## How to compare DVI software: 8 criteria

1. **Technician time per inspection.** Time a real inspection on your own vehicles during the demo. Every extra minute per RO lowers how often techs complete it.
2. **Hands-free capture.** Can techs record findings without taking gloves off or walking to a terminal?
3. **DMS integration.** Confirm the vendor supports your DMS and what data flows both ways. Ask how long setup takes.
4. **Video quality and guidance.** Customer-facing video sells work when it is clear and short. Tools that guide the tech on what to show and say produce more consistent videos.
5. **Warranty documentation.** For franchise dealers, inspections are only half the job. Check whether the tool also helps with 3C stories and warranty claims.
6. **Reporting.** You should see completion rate, items found, items sold, and approval rate by technician and advisor.
7. **Customer experience.** Open a sample inspection on your own phone. Is it easy to read and approve?
8. **Pricing model.** Per rooftop, per technician, or per RO pricing changes the math. Ask for total cost at your current volume.

## Questions to ask in every demo

- What is the average inspection completion rate across your dealer customers?
- How long does a technician take to complete a full MPI with photos?
- Can we run a pilot with two or three technicians before rolling out?
- What happens to our inspection data if we cancel?
- Who handles training, and how long does onboarding take?
