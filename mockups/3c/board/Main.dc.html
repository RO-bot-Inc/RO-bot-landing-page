Your techs fix the car. Then they have to write about it.

Thin stories get kicked back. Somebody rewrites them, the claim waits, and sometimes the dealership eats the labor.

FAQs

What is a 3C story?
A 3C story is the write-up a technician adds to each repair line: the concern (what the customer reported, also called the complaint), the cause (what the tech found and how they confirmed it) and the correction (what was repaired or replaced, and how the fix was verified). Warranty administrators submit it with the claim, and the manufacturer uses it to decide whether to pay.
Complaint or concern: which is it?
Both mean the same thing. Some automakers say complaint, some say concern. TenthGear labels the sections complaint, cause and correction.
What makes a warranty story get paid?
A reviewer wants to see that the concern was verified, how it was diagnosed, the specific part, the root cause, the repair, and proof the repair worked. TenthGear's grader checks a warranty story for exactly those six things.
How is a story graded?
Each criterion gets a letter grade, and the story gets an overall grade. A missing item shows as a dash with a suggestion for what to add. The tech adds a note, rewrites, and the grade updates.
What is Guide me?
On a phone, a warranty line has a Guide me button beside Write Story. It asks one short question at a time with answers to tap, or the tech can speak or type their own. Each answer is saved as a story note, and when the six warranty criteria are covered, Write Story runs as usual.
Does the AI make anything up?
No. The story is written from the tech's notes and the line on the RO. Guide me never guesses a fault code, a reading or a part number: the tech says it, or it comes from the notes or a scan-tool photo. Every section stays editable before it's copied.
Does it write customer-pay stories too?
Yes. Customer-pay stories are graded on five criteria, including plain language and a professional tone, because the customer reads them. Scheduled maintenance lines are graded on four.
How does the story get into our DMS?
Each section has a Copy button, so the tech or the warranty admin pastes complaint, cause and correction into the DMS. On Reynolds, there are buttons for the CC and M# screens.
Is TenthGear integrated with our DMS?
TenthGear currently integrates with Auto/Mate, Tekion, CDK, Reynolds, Dealertrack, PBS, Quorum, Autosoft, DealerBuilt and Dominion. DMS integration isn't required for all implementations.
