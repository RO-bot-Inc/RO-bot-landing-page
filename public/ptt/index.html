TenthGear push-to-talk handheld test

Chrome or Edge only. First pair the handheld to this computer in the Bluetooth settings and note the name it shows there. Then click Connect, pick that same name in the list, and allow the microphone. Hold the handheld's button, talk, let go. The checklist below ticks itself. When all six are green, click Send results.

Idle
  • –
    Handheld button connected
  • –
    Handheld microphone detected by the browser
  • –
    Recording with this tab in front
    Hold the button, talk, let go.
  • –
    Recording with another tab in front
    Switch to any other tab, then hold the button, talk, let go. Come back here.
  • –
    Recording after 10 minutes idle
    Leave the laptop alone for 10 minutes, then press and talk once more.
  • –
    Reconnects after the handheld is turned off and on
    Turn the handheld off, wait 10 seconds, turn it on. Wait for the status line to say Ready again, then press and talk.
