Every other labor line on the RO has an owner. Diagnostic time does not.

So the two hours your tech spent chasing an intermittent no-start still land somewhere. Usually on the tech.

Quick one this morning.

Every other labor line on the repair order has an owner. Diagnostic time does not. Depending on your franchise's policy it might be built into the repair labor op, paid under its own allowance, gated behind an authorization nobody got, or not covered at all. When none of those pay, your tech eats the hours or your internal account does.

The part worth your attention is what is happening in state legislatures. The warranty labor rate fight is mostly settled. The fight over labor TIME is just getting started, and a bill pending in New York would set compensation at the actual time taken, as documented by the dealer, where no time allowance exists. It is one bill in one state and it may die in committee. But legislators are now arguing about how warranty labor time gets calculated and documented, not just what the hour is worth. That argument was not happening five years ago.
